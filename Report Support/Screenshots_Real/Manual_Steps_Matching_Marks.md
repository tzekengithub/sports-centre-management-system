# Manual steps that match the numbered marks (Annotated_v2)

Each number below is the number drawn on the picture of the same name in `Annotated_v2/`. Where a step has the same number on two boxes, both boxes belong to that step. Figure 54 (Home) has nothing to click, so it has no marks.

**Figure 55: Booking menu**
1. Click the Booking tab.
2. Click Book a Court.

**Figure 56: Book a Court**
1. Click the Booking tab, then Book a Court.
2. Type the date (or click the calendar button) and click the sport.
3. Click the green hours you want. They turn amber with a tick. Neighbouring hours on one court become one booking.
4. Choose the member in the Member box (type a name or click the arrow).
5. Click Book.

**Figure 57: Reschedule Booking**
1. Click the Booking tab, then Reschedule Booking.
2. Click the booking in the list.
3. Choose the new date, start time and court.
4. Click Reschedule and answer Yes.

**Figure 58: Cancel a Booking**
1. Click the Booking tab, then Cancel a Booking.
2. Click the booking in the list (search by member, booking ID or date if needed).
3. Pick Cancel from and Cancel until. By default the whole booking is selected.
4. Click Cancel Booking and answer Yes.

**Figure 59: View All Bookings**
1. Click the Booking tab, then View All Bookings.
2. To find a booking, type in the search box (booking ID, member ID or name, date, sport or status) and click Search.
3. Click a row to see its details on the right.

**Figure 60: Membership menu**
1. Click the Membership tab.
2. Click Register.

**Figure 61: Register a Member**
1. Click the Membership tab, then Register.
2. Type the member's name and phone number.
3. Click the membership card you want.
4. Click Register.

**Figure 62: Manage Members**
1. Click the Membership tab, then Manage Members.
2. Click a member in the list. Their details appear in the edit fields on the right; change the name, phone or type.
3. Click Save Changes to save, or click Delete beside a member to remove them (answer Yes; a member with an upcoming booking, an unpaid bill or an upcoming class can't be deleted).

**Figure 63: Renew Membership**
1. Click the Membership tab, then Renew Membership.
2. Click the member in the list.
3. Click Renew. A message shows the new expiry date.

**Figure 64: Coaching menu**
1. Click the Coaching tab.
2. Click Manage Coaches.

**Figure 65: Manage Coaches**
1. Click the Coaching tab, then Manage Coaches.
2. To add a coach, click New for a blank form, fill in Name, Phone, Sport, Hourly rate and Commission, then click Add Coach. (On this picture a coach is already picked, so the button reads Save Changes; after New it reads Add Coach.)
3. To change a coach, click their row, edit the form and click Save Changes.
4. Click Delete beside a coach to remove them (answer Yes; a coach with only past classes becomes Inactive).

**Figure 66: Manage Classes**
1. Click the Coaching tab, then Manage Classes.
2. To schedule a class, click New for a blank form, choose the coach and type (Private or Group), then fill in date, start time, duration, max people and fee, and click Schedule Class. (On this picture a class is already picked, so the button reads Save Changes; after New it reads Schedule Class.)
3. To change a class, click its row, edit the form and click Save Changes.
4. Click Delete beside a class to cancel it (answer Yes).

**Figure 67: Enrol Member**
1. Click the Coaching tab, then Enrol Member.
2. Click the class in the list.
3. Choose the member in the Member box.
4. Click Enrol.

**Figure 68: Remove Member**
1. Click the Coaching tab, then Remove Member.
2. Click the class and member row in the list.
3. Click Remove and answer Yes.

**Figure 69: Hours & Commission**
1. Click the Coaching tab, then Hours & Commission.
2. Choose All time or a month.
3. To look at one coach, type their name and click Search.

**Figure 70: Billing menu**
1. Click the Billing tab.
2. Click Create Bill.

**Figure 71: Create a Bill**
1. Click the Billing tab, then Create Bill.
2. Choose the member, then choose what the bill is for.
3. Choose the booking (or class) to bill. For a retail sale type the item and price instead.
4. Check the amount, discount and total, then click Create Bill.

**Figure 72: Pay a Bill**
1. Click the Billing tab, then Pay Bill.
2. Click the unpaid bill in the list.
3. Choose Cash, Card or E-Wallet and type the amount received.
4. Click Pay.

**Figure 73: Void a Bill**
1. Click the Billing tab, then Void Bill.
2. Click the unpaid bill in the list.
3. Click Void Bill and answer Yes.

**Figure 74: View a Receipt**
1. Click the Billing tab, then View Receipt.
2. Click the paid bill in the list.
3. Click View Receipt. The receipt is shown in a message.

**Figure 75: Daily Revenue**
1. Click the Billing tab, then Daily Revenue.
2. Type the date (or click the calendar button).
3. Click Show. The table also updates by itself once the date is complete.
