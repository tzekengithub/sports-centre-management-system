Screenshots: where each picture goes

How to use
  1. Go to your name below, or open your folder next to this file.
  2. In the Google Doc, find the yellow [Paste screenshot here] line (use Ctrl+F for the figure number, such as "Figure 56").
  3. Delete the yellow line and paste the picture with Insert > Image > Upload from computer. Pictures should be about 15 cm wide.
  4. Check that the caption under it matches the "Caption to type" below. Tick the Done box (it is remembered in this browser).

Folder A4_System_pages = Section A.4 (system GUI). Folder C3_User_manual_with_arrows = Section C.3 (user manual, red circles and arrows already added; Figure 54 is plain).
